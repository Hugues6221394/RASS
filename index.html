Rwanda Agri Stability System (RASS)

Architecture, district-level price regulation, and out-of-sample evaluation of market price forecasting on WFP Rwanda retail prices

Summary

RASS is a web platform that brings farmers, cooperatives, buyers, market agents, transporters, storage operators and government officials onto one system. It provides national price intelligence, district-level price regulation enforced at the point of listing, and short-term price forecasts with uncertainty intervals and role-specific advice.

Key evaluation result. On 28 WFP retail price series (6 crops, 10 markets, 2018–2026), evaluated out of sample, the platform's statistical ensemble (SARIMA, Prophet, Holt) achieves a mean absolute percentage error of 9.6%. That compares with 11.1% for a naive forecast. It reduces the naive error by 15.4% two months ahead and 22.1% three months ahead (both p < 0.001), and its 80% intervals cover 84–88% of outcomes.

The report also documents the defects found by an independent code audit and their correction. It assesses security and data protection and proposes a roadmap to a supervised pilot.
